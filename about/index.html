About

I am a 4th-year PhD candidate in Operations Research at the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology.

Academic background

I am advised by Professor Hayriye Ayhan. My work studies the structure and optimization of stochastic service systems, especially queueing systems in which customers may abandon before receiving service.

My broader interests include stochastic processes, continuous- and discrete-time Markovian systems, stochastic and dynamic optimization, and exact analysis of Markov decision processes.

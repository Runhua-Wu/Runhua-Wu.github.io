About

Applied probability for service systems.

I am a Ph.D. student in Operations Research at Georgia Tech. My research interests include queueing systems and stochastic processes. I am especially interested in structural properties of optimal policies for admission control, service-rate control, and server scheduling in systems with impatient customers.

Research

Current research directions

My work develops analytical characterizations and computational methods for controlling queueing systems with customer abandonment.

Admission control

Characterizing optimal admission decisions in queueing systems with multiple classes of impatient customers.

Service-rate control

Studying optimal service-rate policies in queues with finite or infinite buffer space and developing efficient algorithms to compute them.

Server scheduling

Investigating optimal non-anticipating scheduling policies for M/G/1+M queueing systems.

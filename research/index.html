Research

Stochastic systems and queueing control

My research focuses on structural and exact analysis of queueing systems with customer abandonments, using tools from stochastic processes, Markov decision processes, and dynamic optimization.

  • Queueing systems
  • Customer abandonments
  • Stochastic processes
  • Markov decision processes
  • Stochastic optimization
  • Dynamic optimization
01

Admission control

I study admission decisions in queueing systems with multiple classes of impatient customers, with an emphasis on complete structural characterizations of optimal policies.

02

Service-rate control

I investigate optimal service-rate policies in queues with finite and infinite buffer space and develop efficient algorithms for computing these policies.

03

Server scheduling

I study non-anticipating server scheduling in queues with abandonments, including M/G/1+M systems and long-run throughput objectives.

04

Exact MDP analysis

I am interested in exact policy analysis for continuous- and discrete-time Markovian systems, rather than relying only on approximations or limiting regimes.

Research perspective

Many service systems are affected by impatience: customers may leave while waiting, so control decisions influence both congestion and abandonment. My work seeks policy structures that are mathematically exact, interpretable, and computationally useful.

Current projects span admission control, service-rate control, and scheduling. See Publications for papers and preprints.
